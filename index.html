Before you join

A clearer next step, without the guesswork.

Can I use the app today?

This site is accepting launch interest. Joining the list does not activate app access. We’ll email you when onboarding is available.

Does joining lock in a price?

No. The launch list is free and carries no purchase obligation. Review confirmed pricing and subscription terms when purchasing.

Will this replace a property manager?

PTI is designed to help hands-on owners organize and streamline rental work. It does not provide every service of a local property manager; decide what support your properties need.

How accurate is the audit?

It is an illustrative workload model. Your portfolio size and hourly rate change the estimate. Actual time, costs, and potential savings depend on how you manage.

What happens to my information?

We use your email and portfolio size to follow up about launch and onboarding. Read our privacy policy for details or contact hello@perfecttenantinnovation.com.
