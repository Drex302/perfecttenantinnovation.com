Use the right response at each stage

Tenant is current but stretchedDiscuss the issue early, document repairs, offer written communication channels, and introduce PTI as the planned ongoing stability layer—not as a source of cash.
First missed paymentSend a factual balance statement, ask whether the tenant is applying for assistance, and provide the owner documents the program requires. Avoid promises that funding is guaranteed.
Assistance is pendingTrack the application separately from legal deadlines. Put any temporary pause or repayment plan in writing, with dates, balances, and what happens if funding is denied.
A case has been filedFollow local court rules and qualified counsel. Keep negotiating if appropriate, but do not assume an application, hotline call, or mediation intake changes a court order.
The account is stabilizedReturn to prevention: consistent communication, documented maintenance, clear expectations, and a PTI workflow when product access becomes available.

Filing, diversion, and mediation workflow

  1. Audit the property first. Confirm ownership authority, rental registration or license, rent-regulation status, voucher or federal coverage, lead compliance, and open repair violations.
  2. Reconcile the ledger. Separate rent from fees, credits, utilities, deposits, and assistance promises. Use the exact recoverable rent figure required by the notice.
  3. Use the current official notice. Select the correct ground, language, cure period, service method, and proof of service from the official source above.
  4. Open the recovery track at the same time. Contact the rent-assistance provider listed above, provide requested owner documents securely, and record whether the application is merely started, complete, approved, or paid.
  5. Complete mandatory diversion. If Tucson requires pre-filing diversion, conciliation, or a landlord certificate, finish it before filing. Otherwise request mediation through the local court or legal provider and document every offer.
  6. File in the court serving the property. For this market that is generally Pima County Justice Court — precinct serving the property. Attach the lease, predicate notice, proof of service, ledger, registration evidence, and any required diversion certificate.
  7. Serve and attend. Follow the court’s summons rules, appear at every conference or hearing, and do not use locks, utility interruption, or property removal outside a court-authorized process.
  8. Document the resolution. Put payment plans, assistance conditions, agreed move-outs, dismissals, and possession terms in a signed writing. Confirm receipt before crediting third-party funds.

Tucson housing-authority owner onboarding

  1. Open the official owner page for City of Tucson Housing Authority and confirm whether new owner packets or unit listings are accepted.
  2. Prepare the deed or management authorization, W-9, direct-deposit form, owner contact details, and proof that local registration is current.
  3. When a voucher holder selects the unit, complete the Request for Tenancy Approval and submit the proposed lease and rent.
  4. Respond to rent-reasonableness review and schedule the required inspection. Correct deficiencies and keep invoices and dated completion photos.
  5. Sign the Housing Assistance Payments contract and approved lease documents only after the authority authorizes them.
  6. Use the owner portal for payment history, annual recertification, inspections, ownership changes, and properly noticed rent-change requests.

Why retention economics should come before eviction economics

A recovered balance is only part of the decision. Turnover can add vacancy, cleaning, repairs, marketing, screening, leasing labor, and uncertainty about the next tenancy. When a tenant can resume sustainable payments, a documented recovery plan may protect more cash flow than immediate replacement.

That does not mean every tenancy can or should continue. It means the landlord should compare the full cost of both paths, comply with local law, and use emergency programs for the crisis while building a better operating system for what follows.
