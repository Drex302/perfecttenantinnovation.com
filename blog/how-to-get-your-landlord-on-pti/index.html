How to Get Your Landlord on PTI (And Why They'll Say Yes)

When your landlord joins PTI, your rewards and linked tenant features can activate. Here are three ready-to-send messages — plus objection handlers and your personal invite link workflow.

By Drexton Andrews  ·  ~6 min read  ·  Updated June 2026

Why they'll say yes 3 templates Invite link Objections If they say no

You're one conversation away from activating linked PTI features. A clear, honest message beats a perfect draft you never send — pick a template below and include your invite link.

You signed up for PTI. You saw how PTI Points, optional rent reporting, and Stay Grade can work when your lease is connected on the platform. Then you realized: your landlord usually needs to participate too for the full tenant experience to activate.

That's by design. PTI links tenant and landlord accounts so rent workflows stay verified against your actual lease — and both sides can benefit from documented payments, maintenance, and retention tools.

This guide gives you what you need: the three reasons landlords tend to say yes, three copy-ready message templates for different relationships, how your personal invite link works, what to say when they push back, and what to do if they decline.

Why your landlord will actually want to do this

Before you write a single word, understand what most landlords worry about every month. It's usually three things:

💰

Will rent get paid on time?

PTI rewards consistent on-time payment and documents the pattern — incentives and visibility landlords can use without micromanaging.

📅

Will this tenant stay long-term?

Credit-building and Stay Grade signal commitment. Tenants building a record on PTI have a reason to treat the lease seriously.

🏠

Will my property be taken care of?

Maintenance requests and property check-ins live in one place — fewer surprises and a clearer paper trail when something needs repair.

Your message doesn't need to explain the whole platform. Touch those three anxieties and make them smaller. The templates below are built for that — in as few words as possible.

One more thing your landlord will care about PTI uses membership-style landlord pricing — not a percentage of your rent like many property managers. Confirm current plans on /join/. Lead with the three anxieties above; use pricing only if they ask.

The three message templates

Pick the one that fits your relationship. A genuine message you actually send beats a polished message you never send.

Template 1: The casual text

For landlords you text regularly or have a friendly relationship with

📱 Text / iMessage

Hey [Name] — wanted to mention something. I signed up for PTI, which lets me earn rewards for paying rent on time and can report payments to credit bureaus where that's enabled for my lease.

There are tools on your end too — maintenance tracking, messaging, documents in one place. It's a flat monthly subscription, not a cut of my rent like a property manager.

I have a personal invite link that connects us when you sign up. Want me to send it? I think you'd find it useful.

Why this works: leads with your honest benefit, adds theirs naturally, ends with a low-pressure question.

Template 2: The professional email

For formal relationships, property managers, or anyone who prefers email

✉️ Email

Subject: A platform I think would benefit both of us as tenant and landlord

Hi [Name],

I wanted to share something I recently signed up for called Perfect Tenant Innovation (PTI). It's a tenant rewards and operations platform that I think you'd find useful as a landlord.

For me as a tenant: I earn PTI Points for on-time rent where my program is active, optional rent reporting to credit bureaus when enabled, and incentives to document property condition and maintenance requests.

For you as a landlord: PTI offers maintenance tracking, tenant messaging, and document management at a flat monthly subscription — not a percentage of rent. It pairs with a tenant who is rewarded for paying on time and staying engaged with the property.

I have a personal invite link that connects our accounts when you sign up. I'd be happy to send it or answer any questions.

Thank you,
[Your name]

Why this works: frames both parties' interests equally and ends with an open offer, not a demand.

Template 3: The direct pitch

When you want them on board soon — e.g. you're ready to activate Points and linked features this month

💬 Text or in-person

I want to ask you directly. I signed up for PTI — on-time rent earns me Points I can use in the Shopping Universe, and rent reporting can help my credit file where that's available for my lease.

Most of that activates once you're subscribed too. Your cost is a flat monthly fee — confirm pricing on perfecttenantinnovation.com/join — and you get maintenance, messaging, and tenant tools on your side.

Here's my personal invite link: [your PTI invite link from your dashboard]

I'd appreciate five minutes to look at it. It helps me stay on top of rent and communication, which benefits you as well.

Why this works: honest urgency without sounding desperate — includes the link and closes on mutual benefit.

How the personal invite link works

When you sign up for PTI, you get a personal invite link from your tenant dashboard (often under Invite Your Landlord). It pre-connects your account to your landlord's when they sign up through it — so you don't have to hunt each other down manually later.

1

Sign up and copy your invite link

Find it in your tenant dashboard. It's unique to your account.

2

Send the link using one of the templates above

Text, email, or say it in person and send the link right after.

3

Your landlord signs up through your link

They choose a landlord plan on /join/ — confirm current pricing and features before you pitch a specific dollar amount.

4

Linked tenant features can activate

Your dashboard should show when the connection is live. From there, on-time payments can earn Points, rent reporting may run where enabled, and Stay Grade can build — subject to your program.

What if your landlord has questions?

"Is this legitimate?"

PTI is Perfect Tenant Innovation — transparent subscription pricing at perfecttenantinnovation.com. Landlords pay a flat monthly fee, not a hidden cut of rent. They can review plans on the join page and cancel according to their subscription terms.

"I already use [other software]. Why switch?"

You're not asking them to rip out every tool overnight. The core pitch is a tenant who's incentivized to pay on time, document maintenance, and stay engaged — plus optional ops tools in one place. Frame PTI as additive unless they want to consolidate later.

"Let me think about it" — then silence

Follow up once, 5–7 days later: "Hey — circling back on that PTI link. Happy to answer questions. Here it is again: [link]." One follow-up is appropriate; two is the practical max.

"No, I'm not interested."

That's a business decision about software — not a verdict on you as a tenant. See the next section.

What to do if your landlord says no

A landlord who declines isn't saying PTI doesn't work — they're choosing not to adopt another platform right now. You still have paths forward:

  • Try a different angle once. If you led with tenant rewards first, try landlord ops and flat-fee pricing on a second message — or vice versa.
  • Look for a participating landlord when you move. In active PTI markets, some listings and managers already use the platform — you walk into a connected lease from day one.
  • Keep building your profile. Stay Grade and account history can start before your landlord joins. When they connect later — or when you move — you may already have months of documented behavior. See how to build credit as a renter for the full picture.

The most important step Send one real message this week. Include your invite link. Most landlords respond when the ask is short, mutual, and easy to verify on the website.
