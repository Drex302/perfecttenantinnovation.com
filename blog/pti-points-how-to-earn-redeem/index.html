Rent is the largest monthly payment most renters make. Historically, it’s money that goes out and nothing comes back — no rewards, no reputation, and in many cases no credit-file benefit.

PTI Points change that equation. When you pay rent on time through the PTI platform, you earn points that accumulate in your account and can be redeemed in the PTI Shopping Universe. At the same time, your consistent on-time history can strengthen your Stay Grade — your portable rental reputation signal.

What can you redeem PTI Points for?

The PTI Shopping Universe is a curated marketplace of products and services from local and national merchants who participate in PTI. Redemptions vary by category and availability — but the intent is simple: your points should map to things you actually want.

🏠

Home goods

Décor, kitchen items, small appliances, and practical upgrades that make your space feel like home.

💆

Personal care

Skincare, bath products, hair care, and wellness items from local and national brands.

🍽️

Food & beverage

Specialty foods, snacks, and local brands — especially strong in PTI’s city markets.

👕

Fashion & apparel

Local designers, streetwear, and everyday essentials — depending on merchant participation.

💻

Digital products

Templates, downloads, courses, and digital goods with instant delivery.

✂️

Local services

Select experiences and services in your market (where offered by participating merchants).

Points + cash for higher-value items
When available, points-plus-cash options let you apply your points toward higher-value items and pay the remainder. That helps you redeem sooner without waiting for a full-balance purchase.

Your Stay Grade is the practical layer above points: it packages your consistency into a trust signal. In competitive markets, a verified, sustained on-time record can matter — especially when landlords want less risk and less ambiguity.

Five strategies to maximize your PTI Points balance

  1. Pay 3–5 days early when you can. Small bonuses compound over 12 months.
  2. Protect your streak. Milestone bonuses are usually worth more than one-off actions.
  3. Complete your profile. One-time setup bonuses are free value and stronger context.
  4. Redeem intentionally. Use points on things you’d buy anyway — let the program reduce your real expenses.
  5. Use points-plus-cash when available. It helps you redeem sooner on higher-ticket items.

Frequently asked questions

How do PTI Points work?

Pay rent on time through PTI, earn points automatically, and redeem them in the PTI Shopping Universe. Early payment and program milestones can add bonus points.

What can PTI Points be redeemed for?

Common categories include home goods, personal care, food and beverage, apparel, wellness, digital products, and local experiences — availability varies by merchant and market.

Do PTI Points expire?

PTI Points generally do not expire while your account remains active and in good standing. If your account becomes inactive, policies may vary — check with PTI support.
