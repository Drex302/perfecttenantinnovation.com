PTI for Indianapolis landlords

🏠 Landlord guide · Indianapolis, IN

Indiana’s framework under Title 32 gives Indianapolis landlords a middle-ground notice period for non-payment (10 days when properly executed), a 45-day security deposit return window with itemized deductions, no statewide rent control, and predictable Marion County court practice when notices are clean. A key local nuance is Indianapolis source-of-income protection — verify current ordinance text and enforcement with counsel.

Indiana landlord-tenant law in Indianapolis (educational)

Indiana Code Title 32 governs most residential leases. Indianapolis adds source-of-income themes landlords should verify with counsel:

  • Security deposits: No statutory cap on amount; return within 45 days with itemized deductions when applicable — missing deadlines can forfeit deduction rights.
  • Non-payment: 10-day notice to pay or vacate before filing in Marion County Courts when properly executed.
  • Source of income: Indianapolis ordinance themes require evaluating HCV applicants on standard criteria — confirm with the Office of Equal Opportunity and qualified counsel.
  • No rent control: Indiana preempts local rent control.
  • Entry: Reasonable notice — 24 hours is commonly cited for non-emergency access in Indiana practice.
