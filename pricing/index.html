Billing & cancellation

Joining this list is free and does not create a subscription, reserve a price, or charge your card. Web checkout is disabled. Review the final price, renewal terms, and cancellation options in the app before purchasing.

FAQ

Subscription questions

Can I subscribe here?
No. This page collects launch interest. You will receive availability and onboarding updates before choosing whether to purchase in the app.
Can I reserve or subscribe here?
Yes. Create your Perfect Tenant profile and reserve your plan for free (no card), or lock in your price with $0 due today. Either way you're not charged until app access begins at launch — and web prices are lower than in-app (Apple) pricing.
Which plan should I choose?
The reference tiers are Ascent for 1–5 properties, Summit for 6–50, and Pinnacle for 51+. Properties and rental units are different: tell us your unit count so we can help confirm fit.
Am I committing to a plan?
No. Your selection helps us understand your needs. You can review the final offer before making a purchase.
