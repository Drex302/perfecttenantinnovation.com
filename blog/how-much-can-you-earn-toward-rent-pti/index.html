How Much Can You Earn Toward Rent Through PTI? Real Numbers.

Illustrative earning ranges by follower count, engagement rate, platform, and campaign frequency — so you know what to expect before you start. Not a guarantee.

By Drexton Andrews  ·  ~8 min read  ·  Updated June 2026

Tiers Scenarios Categories Calculator Maximize Full stack

The most common question new PTI members ask is also the most reasonable one: how much can I actually earn? The mistake in answering is giving a number so vague it is useless ("up to hundreds a month!") or so optimistic it sets people up for disappointment.

This post gives grounded scenarios — broken down by follower tier, engagement rate, platform, and how often you run campaigns. These are illustrative ranges from typical campaign structures in PTI's active markets (Birmingham, Atlanta, Houston, Memphis, Indianapolis, Cleveland, Jacksonville, Charlotte, Detroit). Your results will vary.

The short version: many active members who run about two campaigns per month land in roughly $45–$220/month in rent-related rewards, depending on audience size and engagement. Against a $1,200 rent example, that is often about 4–18% — a real offset, not a full rent payment, that can compound with PTI Points, optional rent reporting, and Stay Grade when your landlord is linked.

Before we get into the numbers PTI campaign earnings are rent-related rewards applied through your tenancy workflow when your landlord participates and your program supports it — not cash deposits, gift cards, or bank transfers. PTI is not emergency cash and does not replace rental assistance. Many full features require your landlord to join via your invite link.
