What Swarm Boss campaigns look like in practice

📍 Local Business Launch

Target tenants within 2 miles of your new location. Task: visit the store, take a photo, leave a Google review. Pay only on verified completion.

📱 App Download Campaign

Task: download the app, create a profile, take a screenshot of the home screen. AI verifies the screenshot. Stripe pays instantly.

🏘️ Neighborhood Awareness

Target tenants in specific zip codes. Task: take a photo of your billboard or signage in their neighborhood. Verified proof. Budget only spent on results.

🛍️ Product Sampling

Ship samples to verified tenant addresses. Task: unboxing photo and product review. Built-in verification. Zero wasted samples.
