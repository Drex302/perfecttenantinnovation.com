The “panel + service upgrade” lane in Indianapolis

Near-downtown and midtown neighborhoods have plenty of mid-century housing. That often means older panels, undersized services for modern loads, and renovation scopes where landlords need a licensed partner who can quote permitted work clearly.

How to market it without overpromising
Educate: explain when a service upgrade is a safety and capacity issue, what permits and inspections typically involve, and how you document before/after photos (with customer permission). Avoid blanket claims about specific products or agencies—let the site conditions and authority rules drive the scope.

EV charger (Level 2) requests continue to grow in suburban owner markets; listing it as a named service on your GBP and website helps match that search intent.

Indiana licensing & Indianapolis permits (verify)

Electrical credentials and contracting rules are administered at the state level through the Indiana Professional Licensing Agency (IPLA). What you need depends on whether you are performing work as an employee, a journeyman under supervision, or operating as a contractor—confirm your classification before advertising regulated scopes.

  • State credentials: verify current license types, renewals, and continuing education with IPLA.
  • Local permitting / contractor registration: permitted work in Indianapolis may require registration with the city and permits appropriate to the job—confirm with the City of Indianapolis building department for your workflow.
  • Insurance: general liability is standard; workers’ compensation may be required when you have employees. PMs often ask for COIs up front.

For a multi-state view, see Contractor licensing guide by state—then drill into IPLA for Indiana specifics.

Frequently asked questions

How do electricians get more leads in Indianapolis Indiana?

The most reliable mix is: a strong Google Business Profile for Indianapolis and Marion County searches, systematic review collection, direct relationships with landlords and property managers for recurring portfolio work, listing on the PTI service provider network for rental-owner discovery, and referral relationships with home inspectors and general contractors. Panel upgrades and service upgrades are common in older near-downtown inventory—positioning clearly for permitted work helps close landlord jobs.

How much do electricians charge in Indianapolis Indiana?

Pricing varies by company, scope, materials, and whether the call is after-hours. Most shops publish a dispatch or diagnostic policy, then labor (hourly or task-priced) plus parts. Landlords and PMs usually want itemized estimates and clear permit assumptions. Confirm your own band with local comps rather than copying a single number from the internet.

What license do electricians need in Indianapolis Indiana?

Electrical work in Indiana is regulated at the state level through the Indiana Professional Licensing Agency (IPLA). The exact credential you need depends on whether you are contracting, qualifying a company, or working under supervision. In Indianapolis, permitted work may also require contractor registration and permits through the city’s building department (verify current rules with IPLA and the City of Indianapolis). Property managers typically require general liability insurance and may ask for workers’ compensation documentation.
