Why landlords are the best plumbing customers in Birmingham

Most plumbers chase homeowners. The smartest plumbers in Birmingham build landlord lists.

Here's the math. A homeowner calls you once. They get the job done, they're grateful, and you never hear from them again for three years. A landlord with five rental properties calls you multiple times a year — every drain backup, every water heater failure, every toilet that won't stop running across every unit they own. One landlord relationship is worth five to ten times a single homeowner job over a 12-month period.

Birmingham's rental market amplifies this. The city has a high rental occupancy rate — over 52% of occupied housing units are renter-occupied according to recent census data. That means a significant portion of the housing stock is managed by someone who needs a plumber on call, doesn't want to shop around every time, and will pay a fair rate to a reliable provider they already trust.

The landlord value calculation
A Birmingham landlord with 8 rental units generates an average of 3–5 plumbing service calls per year across their portfolio. At a $200 average ticket, that's $600–$1,000 annually from one relationship — acquired at zero lead cost after the first job. Compare that to paying $50/lead on Angi with a 20% close rate.

Step 1: Own your Google Business Profile for Birmingham plumbing searches

1

Your GBP is your most valuable free marketing asset

When someone in Birmingham types "plumber near me" or "emergency plumber Birmingham AL," the first results they see are the Google Maps 3-pack — three local business profiles with star ratings, phone numbers, and addresses. Getting into that 3-pack requires a fully optimized Google Business Profile.

Most plumbing companies in Birmingham have a GBP. Very few have an optimized one. Here's what optimization actually means:

  • ✓
    Business name includes "plumber" or "plumbing"
    e.g. "Smith Plumbing Birmingham" — the keyword in your name helps ranking
  • ✓
    Primary category: "Plumber" (not "Contractor" or "Home Services")
    The primary category is the most important ranking signal in GBP
  • ✓
    Service area set to include Birmingham, Hoover, Vestavia Hills, Homewood, Irondale
    Add every city you serve — each one expands your search footprint
  • ✓
    All services listed individually with descriptions
    Drain cleaning, water heater replacement, leak repair, pipe repair — each as its own service
  • ✓
    Business description includes "Birmingham," "Alabama," and your top services
    Use your full character limit and include local keywords naturally
  • ✓
    At least 10 photos uploaded (job photos, truck, team)
    Profiles with photos get significantly more calls than those without
  • ✓
    Q&A section populated with your own questions and answers
    Ask and answer "Do you serve Hoover AL?" "Do you offer emergency service?"
  • ✓
    Post weekly updates (completed jobs, tips, offers)
    One post per week is enough to signal activity to Google

Step 2: Build a simple website that ranks for Birmingham plumbing searches

2

You don't need a fancy website — you need a local SEO website

A plumber's website has one job: rank for local searches and convert visitors into calls. You don't need ten pages of content, a blog, or a portfolio gallery. You need a fast-loading site with the right words on it.

The pages that matter for a Birmingham plumbing website:

Homepage

Your homepage headline should include your city and your trade. Something like: "Licensed Plumber in Birmingham, AL — Available 24/7." Include your phone number in large text above the fold. List your core services. Include your license number. Add a Google Maps embed showing your service area.

Service pages (one per service)

Individual pages for each major service dramatically improve your chances of ranking. A page titled "Drain Cleaning Birmingham AL" will outrank a general plumbing page for that specific search. Create pages for: drain cleaning, water heater repair and replacement, leak detection, pipe repair, toilet repair, emergency plumbing, and any specialty services you offer.

Neighborhood pages

Birmingham has distinct neighborhoods and suburbs that people search by name. A page for "Plumber in Hoover AL," "Plumber in Vestavia Hills AL," and "Plumber in Homewood AL" captures searches that a Birmingham-generic page misses. These pages don't need to be long — 400 words with the neighborhood name used naturally throughout, your services listed, and a call to action is enough.

The single most important SEO move for Birmingham plumbers
Get your business listed in local Birmingham directories: the Birmingham Business Alliance directory, the Better Business Bureau Birmingham, and the Alabama Plumbing Contractors Association member directory. Each listing is a backlink. Backlinks from local, relevant sources are a strong signal Google uses to rank local providers.

Step 3: Build your Google review count to 25+

3

Reviews are the closest thing to free advertising that exists

In the Birmingham Google Maps 3-pack, the difference between the first and third position often comes down to review count and recency. A plumber with 47 reviews and a 4.8-star average will almost always outrank one with 11 reviews and a 4.9-star average.

Most plumbers in Birmingham have fewer than 20 Google reviews. Getting to 25+ puts you ahead of the majority of local competition. Here's the system:

Ask at the right moment

The best time to ask for a review is the moment the customer expresses satisfaction — usually right after you've fixed the problem and they see the water running clear or the leak stopped. Don't wait until you've invoiced. Ask while the relief is fresh.

Make it frictionless

Create a short link directly to your Google review page. Google Business Profile → "Get more reviews" generates a shareable URL. Text it to the customer immediately.

What a good review looks like for SEO

Reviews that mention your location and service type carry more weight than generic ones. Without coaching the customer on what to say, the reviews you want look like this:

★★★★★

"Called them for an emergency leak in my rental property in Hoover. They were there in 90 minutes, fixed the pipe under the sink, and charged exactly what they quoted. Professional, clean, and fast. Will use again for all my rentals."

— Marcus T., Google Review

Notice what that review contains organically: "Hoover," "rental property," "emergency," "leak" — all local SEO keywords, all placed naturally by a real customer. That review does more for your local ranking than any ad spend.

How to land a landlord as a long-term client

The first job is an audition. Show up on time or early. Call ahead when you're 30 minutes out. Explain what you're doing in plain language. Leave the work area cleaner than you found it. Provide a written invoice with a clear line-item breakdown. Follow up 48 hours later to confirm everything is holding.

That process — which takes almost no extra time — converts a one-time call into a long-term relationship at a rate that will surprise you.

Alabama plumbing license requirements

Every plumber operating in Birmingham must be licensed through the Alabama Plumbers and Gas Fitters Examining Board. Here's what that means in practice:

  • Journeyman Plumber: 4 years of verified plumbing experience + written exam.
  • Master Plumber: 2 additional years after journeyman + additional exam.
  • Business license: Jefferson County business license required. City of Birmingham business license required for work within city limits.
  • Insurance: General liability and workers' compensation (if you have employees). Most property managers will ask for COI before using you.

Unlicensed work is a liability, not a shortcut
Plumbing work performed without a license in Alabama is subject to fines and can void homeowner and landlord insurance policies. Being licensed isn't just a legal requirement — it's what landlords and property managers look for when they're building a vendor list they can trust.
