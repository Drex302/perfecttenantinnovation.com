Brands pay to reach real audiences. Where PTI campaigns run in your market, that spend can flow toward rent-related rewards — here is how the model works and what you can realistically expect.

When you scroll Instagram or TikTok, you are often looking at content someone was paid to create. Brands fund that because authentic posts from real people convert better than generic ads in many categories.

What many renters do not realize: a small, local audience can still have value — especially for neighborhood food, personal care, and local services. PTI connects that value to your tenancy when brand campaigns are available in your market and your landlord is linked on the platform.

This is not a get-rich-quick scheme and you do not need to become a full-time influencer. It is a structured path: brands run campaigns through PTI's marketing stack (including Swarm Boss community campaigns where enabled), you participate in briefs that fit your profile, and compensation can apply toward rent-related rewards — with honest limits this guide states up front.

The model explained plainly

How money moves — no jargon:

1

A brand wants to reach people in specific communities

A food brand in Birmingham, a personal-care line targeting young professionals in Atlanta, a Houston retailer seeking neighborhood visibility. They want genuine posts from real accounts — not only studio ads.

PTI did not invent brand marketing — it connects it to renters.

2

The brand runs a campaign through PTI

The brand sets audience, city, platform, and format. PTI matches eligible tenant members whose profiles fit — when campaigns exist in your market.

You do not cold-pitch brands; you opt into matched briefs.

3

You create an authentic post

You get a brief — brand, message, guidelines. You post in your voice. FTC-style disclosure language is included in campaign requirements when applicable.

Best-performing posts sound like you, not ad copy.

4

Rewards apply on your PTI account

After verification and campaign completion, earned value can credit toward your rent workflow on PTI when your landlord is subscribed and your program supports it — confirm how rewards display in your dashboard.

Not a separate gift card you must cash out manually.

Why this differs from random "paid post" apps Many apps pay tiny amounts, require huge followings, or pay in points that never touch housing costs. PTI's angle is tying marketing spend to a renter's actual lease stack — rent rewards, PTI Points, optional credit reporting, Stay Grade — when your program and landlord support those features.

What an actual campaign post can look like

📸

Instagram photo

You try a local hot sauce, cook a normal meal, post the dish with the product visible, tag the brand, include required disclosure.

"Finally found a hot sauce that works on eggs. [Brand] has been in rotation two weeks. #ad @brand"

🎵

TikTok short video

A personal-care brief for a specific city. You show routine or results in ~30 seconds with disclosure.

"Tried this leave-in — honest first impression →" + #sponsored

👥

Facebook community

A local restaurant wants neighborhood visibility. Your 150 local friends may beat 5,000 random followers for that brief.

Paid disclosure + genuine visit recap — same tone you'd use for friends.

Brands on PTI typically want authenticity — not polished ad scripts.
