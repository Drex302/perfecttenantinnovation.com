Rental Property Investing Memphis Tennessee: The Cash Flow Capital of the US

By Drexton Andrews, Founder of PTI  ·  9 min read  ·  Updated April 2026

Memphis is consistently ranked the top cash flow rental market in the United States. Low property acquisition costs, strong rental demand driven by one of the highest renter-occupied rates in the South, no state income tax in Tennessee, landlord-friendly eviction timelines, and one of the largest HCV tenant pools in the mid-South combine to make Memphis uniquely attractive for independent landlords and out-of-state investors.

This guide covers what makes Memphis work as an investment market, which neighborhoods offer the best risk-adjusted returns, and what infrastructure Memphis landlords need to manage efficiently from any location.

Why Memphis Produces the Country's Best Cash Flow

The Memphis cash flow thesis is straightforward: low acquisition prices combined with strong rental demand create rent-to-price ratios that are nearly impossible to find in coastal markets. A $100,000 property renting for $950/month generates an 11.4% gross yield before expenses — something that would require $400,000+ in acquisition cost to achieve in Atlanta, Charlotte, or Tampa.

Tennessee has no state income tax on wages or salaries (investment income is taxed separately), making Memphis especially attractive for investors who are also residents. Even for out-of-state investors, the combination of high yield and low acquisition cost produces cash-on-cash returns that justify the remote management complexity.

Best Memphis Neighborhoods for Rental Investment

Midtown Memphis

Memphis's most walkable neighborhood with strong professional tenant demand. Higher acquisition costs than East Memphis but strong appreciation trajectory and lower vacancy. Best for landlords seeking tenant stability over maximum yield.

Cordova / East Shelby County

Suburban Memphis with school-driven family rental demand. Higher rents ($1,100–$1,400+), well-maintained inventory, lower crime. Best for landlords seeking quality tenants willing to pay premiums for suburban amenities.

Frayser / Raleigh

Memphis's highest-yield neighborhoods — acquisition costs of $60,000–$90,000 with rents of $750–$950/month producing 10–14% gross yields. Higher management intensity, stronger Section 8 tenant concentration. Best for experienced landlords comfortable with higher-touch management.

Orange Mound / South Memphis

Historically significant Black neighborhood with significant HCV tenant demand. Very low acquisition costs ($40,000–$80,000), strong cash flow, requires proactive maintenance management. Best for landlords with local management infrastructure or a reliable local PM relationship.

Tennessee: Landlord-Friendly Laws Matter for Memphis

Tennessee's eviction process is one of the fastest in the country — 3–6 weeks from first notice to lockout in uncontested cases. No statewide rent control. No security deposit cap. Late fees are unregulated. For Memphis landlords managing high-volume portfolios in neighborhoods with higher turnover risk, Tennessee's legal framework provides real protection that directly affects operating costs.

Memphis's out-of-state investor population is among the highest of any US market — investors from California, New York, and the Pacific Northwest routinely acquire Memphis rentals for cash flow while their local markets offer negative or near-zero returns. Census data confirms Memphis's renter-occupied rate remains among the highest in the mid-South, sustaining demand across all price points.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Memphis a good place to invest in rental property?

Memphis is consistently ranked among the top 3–5 US markets for rental property cash flow. Low acquisition costs, strong rental demand driven by a high renter-occupied rate, no state income tax in Tennessee, and landlord-friendly eviction law make it uniquely attractive. The tradeoff is higher management intensity and lower appreciation compared to growth markets.

What is a good cap rate for Memphis rental property?

Cap rates of 8–12% on single-family rentals in Memphis are common in 2026 — significantly above the 4–6% typical in major coastal markets. Higher-yield neighborhoods (Frayser, South Memphis) can produce 12–15% gross yields at acquisition but require more proactive management. Cordova and East Memphis trade at lower yields (6–8%) with more stable tenant profiles.

What is the best neighborhood to buy rental property in Memphis?

The \"best\" Memphis neighborhood depends on your investor profile. For maximum yield: Frayser/Raleigh or South Memphis. For tenant stability and appreciation: Midtown or Cordova. For Section 8/HCV focus: Orange Mound, South Memphis, and parts of North Memphis have the highest HCV tenant concentration and most consistent demand.
