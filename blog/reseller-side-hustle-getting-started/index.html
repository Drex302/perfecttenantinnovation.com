Reselling is buying at one price and selling higher. The model is simple; the skill is knowing what to buy, where to source, and where demand already exists. Most beginners lose money by stocking up before those answers are clear.

What this guide covers
Sourcing channels, category table, platform comparison, toy profit calculator, 30-day launch checklist, PTI Shopping Universe as a local complement to national marketplaces, and tax FAQ (general info only).

What reselling is—and why it works as a side hustle

Your first inventory can come from your own closets and storage—zero sourcing cost while you learn listings, photos, shipping, and buyer messages. Skills compound: comps research, condition grading, and platform reputation matter more than raw hours over time.

Income ranges in blog posts are illustrative, not promises. Track your own spreadsheet from week one.

Pricing for profit

Work backward from fees, shipping, packaging, and a minimum net $ target per item—not from hope.

Reseller profit calculator (toy)

Minimum profit rule
Set a dollar floor (many part-timers use roughly $15–$20+ net per item as a sanity check). If an item cannot clear it after fees and shipping, leave it on the rack.

30-day launch checklist

  1. Week 1: Audit home inventory; pick one primary platform; list 10 items with 5-photo discipline.
  2. Week 2: One thrift trip—comps on phone before buying; ship first sales fast for reviews.
  3. Week 3: Spreadsheet: buy price, sale price, fees, net, days to sell.
  4. Week 4: Double down on what your data supports; apply at /merchants/ if PTI fits your metro.

Frequently asked questions

How much money can you make reselling as a side hustle?

It varies widely. Beginners often earn modest net profit while learning; experienced sellers can scale with category depth and discipline. Track your own numbers—do not treat internet income bands as guarantees.

What is the best thing to resell to make money?

Usually a category you already understand. Use sold comps before every purchase and specialize before you expand.

Do I need to report reselling income on my taxes?

Resale profit is generally taxable in the U.S. Platforms may issue 1099-K above thresholds. Consult a tax professional—this is not tax advice.
