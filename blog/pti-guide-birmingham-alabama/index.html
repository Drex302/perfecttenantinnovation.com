Alabama landlord-tenant law in Birmingham (URLTA orientation)

Jefferson County has adopted the Alabama Uniform Residential Landlord and Tenant Act (URLTA). That means stronger tenant-protection rules apply in Birmingham than in non-URLTA counties. This is educational only — not legal advice.

  • Security deposit cap: one month’s rent (standard deposits)
  • Deposit return timing: 35 days from move-out with itemized deductions
  • Entry notice: generally 48 hours for non-emergency entry
  • Nonpayment notice: written demand before filing
  • Self-help eviction: illegal — follow court process
  • Habitability: maintain systems and comply with applicable codes
