Housing Authority of the Birmingham District (HABD) — Section 8 and public housing

HABD administers Housing Choice Vouchers (Section 8) and public housing for Birmingham and Jefferson County. For long-term affordable housing, HABD is the right starting point — with multi-year waitlists when the list is even open.

1

Check waitlist status

HABD's HCV waitlist opens and closes by funding and capacity. When closed, applications are not accepted — contact HABD directly for current status.

2

Apply when the waitlist opens

Applications online and in person. Have income documentation, household composition, and current address ready.

3

Wait for your place

Multi-year waits are common. Keep contact info updated — missed notices can cost your spot.

4

Voucher issued → housing search

Limited time to find a qualifying unit. Must pass HQS inspection; landlord must accept the voucher voluntarily — Alabama has no source-of-income mandate.

Alabama tenant rights: what Birmingham renters need to know

Alabama has not adopted the Uniform Residential Landlord and Tenant Act (URLTA) — tenant protections are weaker than in many states. See our Alabama tenant rights guide for deeper context. Summary for Birmingham:

Security deposit

No statutory cap. Return within 60 days with itemized deductions — failure may support a double-damages claim.

Eviction notice

Typically 7 days for non-payment before filing; 7 days to cure or vacate for lease violations.

Habitability

Landlords must maintain fit, habitable units. Document issues in writing.

No rent control

No statewide or local rent stabilization — increases with proper notice (often 30 days).

No SOI protection

Landlords may refuse Section 8 / HCV vouchers — participation is voluntary.

Illegal lockout

No self-help eviction — changing locks without a court order is illegal. Contact legal aid or police.

Documentation matters more in Alabama

Without URLTA's automatic statutory framework, written records — maintenance requests, lease terms, landlord communications — often decide disputes. Keep copies of everything.
