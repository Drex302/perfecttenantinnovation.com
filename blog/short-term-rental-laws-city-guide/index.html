Not legal advice — verify before you list
Short-term rental law is local, parcel-specific, and volatile. Articles (including this one) go stale. Always confirm current rules with your municipality, county, state revenue agencies, HOA, and counsel before advertising a property on Airbnb, VRBO, or similar platforms.

Overview: the national trend (why verification matters)

Across the U.S., the policy arc is familiar: STR volume rises, neighbors complain, housing-cost debates intensify, then cities add permits, fees, inspections, caps, or primary-residence tests. Two addresses in the same metro can face different answers because of zoning district, HOA covenants, corporate limits, or incorporated vs. unincorporated boundaries.

One workflow that saves money
Before you spend on furnishings, pull the official STR applicant checklist for your exact address, then call your insurer about STR coverage. Getting those two answers early prevents the expensive “list first, discover a ban later” mistake.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a license for Airbnb in Houston Texas?

Do not trust a blog for the final answer. Confirm with the City of Houston (and any applicable jurisdiction) for your parcel. Separately, plan on Texas hotel occupancy tax and local hotel tax registration with the Comptroller unless a CPA confirms you are exempt. Read HOA documents even when city rules look light.

Are short-term rentals legal in Charlotte NC?

Many STRs operate legally when fully permitted and taxed—but Charlotte’s official requirements are detailed. Use Charlotte / Mecklenburg government sources for your zoning district before you list, and budget time for any inspection or notification steps described in current materials.

What taxes do I owe on Airbnb income?

Expect multiple layers: local lodging or occupancy, state sales or hotel-type taxes where applicable, and federal income tax on net profit after deductible expenses. Platforms may automate part of the stack—not all of it. A CPA who handles STR should reconcile platform reports with city and state filings.
