The honest picture in 2026

The City of Dallas Social Services page currently says rental-assistance applications are not being accepted. Its intake line still handles utility assistance and referrals, subject to eligibility and available funds. That means renters should not wait on a closed portal.

Call 211 and Dallas 311 for current partner programs, contact Legal Aid of NorthWest Texas immediately after any notice, and check DHA separately for long-term subsidized housing. Specialized city programs remain available for some households, including HOPWA assistance.

Eviction deadlines move fastTexas eviction citations can lead to a hearing quickly. Assistance calls do not extend the answer or hearing deadline. Contact Legal Aid of NorthWest Texas immediately, read every page of the citation, and attend court unless the court itself confirms a change.

Housing vouchers and affordable housing

DHA, Housing Solutions for North Texas
Vouchers & affordable housing
Create a DHA Live account to apply when eligible waiting lists open and to track an existing application. Lists may open by program or property.
Phone: (214) 951-8316

Voucher and property waiting lists are long-term tools, not emergency rent help. Apply during every eligible opening and keep your contact information updated.

What can PTI do for you in this situation?

PTI isn’t emergency cash, and it can’t pay a bill that’s due this week. It works once your landlord joins too, which is free for you. Here’s where it fits.

“I’m behind on rent right now, or I got an eviction notice.”

PTI isn’t the right tool yet. Get emergency help first: call 211 and Legal Aid of NorthWest Texas at (214) 748-1234, the same day you get court papers. Once things settle, PTI is built to help keep you from ending up here again.

“I got help this month, but I’m worried about next month.”

This is where PTI is designed to fit. If you’re already talking with your landlord about catching up, ask them about PTI. Once they join, eligible rent reporting can help build your payment history, and eligible missions and campaigns can earn rewards.

“I’m waiting on the Section 8 list.”

Use the wait. Invite your current landlord to PTI, and your on-time payments from then on can start building a verified rental record, so you’re a stronger applicant when your name comes up.

“I’m moving and need a fresh start.”

Make PTI part of your new lease. Ask your new landlord about PTI when you sign. From then on, your Stay Grade builds a portable rental reputation that follows you to your next home.

“My landlord won’t make repairs.”

PTI can’t force repairs. Report the problem in writing, keep dated photos, and contact local code enforcement or legal aid.

How to apply: get ready before you call

Most programs ask for the same core documents. Having them ready can save days.

Your lease
Proof of household income
